# Privacy Policy for luv-byte

Last updated: 2026-08-15

luv-byte is a browser extension that displays a custom image on top of the
like (heart) button on X (Twitter). This extension does not collect,
store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data We Do Not Collect
luv-byte does not collect, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable
information, health information, financial information, authentication
credentials, personal communications, location data, web browsing history,
user activity, or website content.

## Local Storage Only
Your chosen image, image size, accent color, and on/off setting are saved
using the browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own
device only. This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to
any server operated by the developers or any third party.

## Third-Party Sharing
We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect
any data in the first place.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, please open an issue at:
